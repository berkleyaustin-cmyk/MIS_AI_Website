Malware and Cyber Threats

WannaCry Cyber Attack Example

During May 2017, WannaCry, a ransomeware cryptoworm, spread across Windows computures worldwide. This attack encrypted files and demanded ransom payments in return. It disrupted businesses and healthcare services, becoming a significant example of the dangers of cyber attacks.

Types of Malware

  1. Adware: Unwanted software that floods screen with pop-up ads.
  2. Spyware: Hidden software that tracks online activity, screen actions, and data.

Common Hacker Motives

  1. Gaining unauthorized access to computers.
  2. Financial gain.

Prevention Strategies

  • Install reputable antivirus/antimalware programs.
  • Store important files in an offline or cloud storage separate from your main network.
